StellarFITS User Guide

Astronomy image processing for smart telescopes · version 0.9 beta

StellarFITS turns the stacked FITS file from your smart telescope into a finished picture. It recognises the target, gives it an automatic starting look tuned for that kind of object (nebula, galaxy, star cluster or the Moon), and then lets you fine-tune every part of it with live sliders. Your FITS files are never changed - StellarFITS only reads them and saves new pictures.

Getting started

Install StellarFITS with the installer from stellarfits.co.uk - the installation guide there shows each step, including the Windows "SmartScreen" warning that appears because this beta isn't signed yet. Then open StellarFITS from the Start menu or the desktop.

The first start: StarNet2 and GraXpert

StellarFITS works on its own. Two free programs by other authors make it better, and StellarFITS uses them automatically when they are installed:

  • StarNet2 separates the stars from the nebula so each is processed on its own - smaller, cleaner stars and a stronger nebula. The stars are always put back. Recommended.
  • GraXpert removes light pollution and gradients, and adds AI noise reduction.

Download opens the program's own website. After installing it, press Check again - no restart needed. You can open this window any time from Tools → StarNet2 and GraXpert.

Your first picture

  1. Open a file. Click Open. StellarFITS shows the folder as a grid of previews with the target, date and exposure of each stack - double-click one. Use the stacked FITS file from your telescope (the linear stack), not the JPG it also saves. You can also drag a FITS file onto the StellarFITS shortcut.
  2. Check the target. The target's name and type appear at the top of the side panel. If StellarFITS doesn't recognise it, the lists open by themselves - pick the target, or just the type of object.
  3. Pick a starting look. Under the picture: Natural, Balanced (already chosen), Vivid and Soft, plus your own presets for this target (marked ★). Each tile is a preview of your picture.
  4. Fine-tune with the sliders. Every change shows in the preview after a moment. Press O (or Show original) to compare with the file as it came from the telescope. Ctrl+Z undoes a change.
  5. Export. Click Export (the highlighted button is always the next step), choose the size and information text, then where to save. Exports are always processed at full resolution.
The first preview takes 15-30 seconds when StarNet2 is used; after that, most slider changes show in a second or two because the slow steps are remembered.

The sliders

Simple shows the eight controls that matter most. Rest the mouse on an ⓘ for a short explanation, and double-click a slider to put it back to the preset's value.

SliderWhat it does
PopOne control for more impact: colour, contrast, clarity and sharpness together, and a darker sky.
BrightnessHow far the faint parts are brought up.
Nebula / galaxy brightnessBrightens the subject without brightening the sky (needs StarNet2).
Black pointHow dark the sky ends up. Lower = blacker sky; too low hides faint detail.
ColourOverall colour strength.
WarmthCooler (left) or warmer (right) - e.g. golden galaxy cores.
StarsHow bright the stars are; Star style gives quick looks (Natural, Reduced, Minimal, Bright stars, Starless).
Noise reductionSmooths grain and colour blotches, mostly in the dark sky.

Advanced adds folded sections - click a ▸ heading to open it. A • on a heading means something inside differs from the preset.

  • More colour - Tint (green-magenta; smart telescopes often lean magenta), extra red (hydrogen) and blue, Star colour balance (removes the camera's colour cast), Star colour.
  • More detail - Clarity (mid-size structure: filaments, dust lanes, galaxy arms) and its size, Sharpening (finest detail, never the stars or sky), Contrast curve, Highlight protection (keeps bright cores like Orion's from going white).
  • More stars - Star size reduction, Remove faint stars, Calm stars over nebula (for crowded Milky Way fields), Star halo reduction, Diffraction spikes.
  • More noise - AI noise reduction (GraXpert) and Calm colour noise (red/blue blotches in faint areas).
  • Background - how much of GraXpert's gradient model is removed.
  • Processing - Crop stacking edges, and the StarNet2 / GraXpert switches.

Tools → Settings has the start mode (Simple / Advanced), the light or dark look, and a switch to hide the ⓘ tips once you know the sliders.

Looking closer

The preview is made from a smaller copy of your image, which hides pixel noise. Hold Ctrl and scroll (or use the zoom slider at the bottom right of the picture) to zoom, drag to pan. Turn on 100% detail to see a part of the picture at true full size, processed exactly like the export - the best place to judge noise and sharpening.

Exporting

  • Size: full size, 4K, 2560, 2048 (social media), 1600 (email / web) or 1080 pixels on the long edge.
  • JPEG quality, or save as PNG, or 16-bit TIFF for further editing in another program.
  • Information text: target name, exposure, date, telescope, gain and filter, and your own text (name, location...), with its position and size.

Your choices are remembered. With StarNet2, a full-size export takes a few minutes - the window shows what it is doing.

My presets

Found a look you like? Click Save… next to My presets: type a name for a new preset, or pick one of yours to replace (presets for the current target are at the top, marked ★). A preset keeps every slider plus the object type and the processing switches.

Choose a preset from the My presets list to apply it to any picture. Presets made for a target also appear in the starting looks whenever you open that target again.

Presets are kept in your Windows user folder - Tools → Settings → Open presets folder to back them up or share one.

Batch and Combine

Batch process a folder

Tools → Batch process a folder processes every FITS file in a folder: each with its own target's look (Automatic), with one of your presets, or with the current slider settings. Files are named by target, date and exposure. Skip images already done lets you stop and carry on later.

Combine stacks

Tools → Combine stacks joins several stacks of the same target - from different nights, with field rotation - into one deeper stack. It lines them up by their stars, evens out their backgrounds and gives cleaner stacks more weight. The result is saved as a new FITS file and opened. (The Moon can't be combined this way: it has no stars to line up.)

The Moon

Open a Moon stack and StellarFITS finds the disc - full, gibbous, half or crescent - frames it on a black sky, removes the colour cast many Moon stacks have, and brings out the craters without burning out the bright highlands. Only the controls that apply are shown, some with Moon names:

ControlFor the Moon
Crater detailCrisper small craters and fine surface detail.
Surface contrastLarger shapes - maria edges, ray systems, the terminator.
Mineral colourThe Moon's real colour: far left = neutral grey, far right shows the bluish and brownish maria clearly.
Sky brightnessThe sky around the Moon; 0 = pure black.

Starting looks: Natural grey, Balanced, Detailed and Mineral colour. Frame the Moon (Processing) can be unticked to keep the whole frame.

Tips and problems

If…Try this
Target not recognisedPick it from the Target list, or just choose the object type - the look comes from the type.
Sky looks grey or blotchyLower Black point a little; raise Noise reduction; for red/blue patches in faint areas, raise Calm colour noise (More noise).
Picture leans pink / magentaMove Tint (More colour) slightly to the right.
Bright core burnt outRaise Highlight protection (More detail) and lower Brightness.
Too many stars over the nebulaCalm stars over nebula or Star style → Reduced.
Export is slowNormal with StarNet2 (a few minutes at full size). GraXpert's AI noise reduction is much slower without a supported graphics card - set it to 0 if you don't need it.
Coloured band at an edgeKeep Crop stacking edges ticked (Processing).
Swirly or streaky backgroundA pattern from the camera that dark frames normally remove - take a fresh dark library at the temperature you shoot at.

Help and feedback

This is a beta, and your feedback shapes the next version. Tools → Send feedback opens an email with the app and PC details already filled in (or copies them, if you use webmail). A screenshot helps a lot - and if a file caused the problem, the FITS file too (a OneDrive or Google Drive link is fine).

New versions: when one is out, a small coral New version link appears at the top right of the window (StellarFITS checks at most once a day, and sends nothing about you or your files). Click it to see what's new and download it; your settings and presets are kept. Tools → Check for updates checks straight away, and Tools → Settings can switch the automatic check off.
